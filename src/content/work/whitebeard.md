## The brief

We built Whitebeard's first website, and as the firm grew it needed a site to match. Whitebeard has been AAT licensed since 2009 and works with ambitious London companies, so the new site had to feel premium and established while staying approachable for growing businesses.

## The approach

We started again with a custom-coded build: a deep navy and gold palette, elegant serif headlines and plenty of space, set against the London skyline the firm works across. Services, industries and pricing each get their own clear pages, with a blog of plain-English guides and a free consultation that can be booked in a couple of clicks.

## The result

A complete overhaul that takes Whitebeard from a simple brochure site to a full, premium website that is fast on every device and easy to grow. The new site is in its final checks before launch.
