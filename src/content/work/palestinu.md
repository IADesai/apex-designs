## The brief

Palestinu is a Palestinian-owned chocolate brand based in Oxford, with bars designed by Palestinian hands in Gaza. They needed a website that tells the story behind every bar and sells it directly to customers, without the brand getting lost in a generic shop template.

## The approach

We built a bold, dark design around Palestinu's keffiyeh hummingbird emblem, letting the colours of the brand and the packaging do the talking. The homepage splits visitors between the brand's story and the shop, so people who want to learn and people who want to buy both find their way in one click.

Behind the shop sits a full e-commerce and order fulfilment system. Each chocolate has its own product page with size and quantity options, from a single bar up to larger boxes. Orders, payments and dispatch are handled in one place, so the team can see what needs packing and sending at a glance.

## The result

A brand site and online shop in one, giving Palestinu a direct way to sell to customers across the UK and a simple process for getting every order out of the door.
